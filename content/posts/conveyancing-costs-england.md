There is no single conveyancing fee in England. The number to compare is the total: the firm's legal fee, VAT at 20% on that fee, and the disbursements the firm pays to other people for you. Buyers also pay Stamp Duty Land Tax to HMRC. The tax does not change with the firm, so it is not a reason to choose one quote over another. You can [compare conveyancing fees in England](/conveyancing/england/) with VAT and disbursements in each total.

## What goes into the quote?

A complete quote has three parts, and then the tax.

1. **The legal fee.** This is the firm's charge for its own work: checking the title, raising enquiries, reporting to you and your lender, exchanging contracts and completing. Most firms quote a fixed fee that rises with the price. Firms regulated by the [Solicitors Regulation Authority](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/transparency/) must publish residential conveyancing prices, including what is and is not covered. Licensed conveyancers, regulated by the [Council for Licensed Conveyancers](https://www.clc-uk.org/), publish in the same way.
2. **VAT** at 20% on the legal fee. Check whether the figure you were shown already includes it.
3. **Disbursements.** These are payments to other people: searches, HM Land Registry fees, identity checks and a bank transfer on completion. [Conveyancing disbursements explained](/blog/conveyancing-disbursements-explained/) sets out the official fees.

Stamp Duty Land Tax is usually collected by your conveyancer and paid to HMRC. It is a tax, not part of the legal fee. [How to read a conveyancing quote](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/) covers the extras that a short quote sometimes leaves out.

| Part of what you pay | Who sets it | The same for every firm? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal fee | The firm | No. This is the figure to compare |
| VAT at 20% | Charged on the legal fee | The rate is fixed. The amount follows the fee |
| Searches and HM Land Registry fees | Councils, search providers and HM Land Registry | Largely yes, for the same house |
| Stamp Duty Land Tax | HMRC | Yes |

## How much is Stamp Duty Land Tax?

From 1 April 2025, Stamp Duty Land Tax on a main home in England is charged in slices. You pay each rate only on the part of the price in that band. The bands below are from [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates), checked 21 November 2026.

| Part of the price | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £125,000 | 0% |
| £125,001 to £250,000 | 2% |
| £250,001 to £925,000 | 5% |
| £925,001 to £1.5 million | 10% |
| Over £1.5 million | 12% |

GOV.UK's worked example is a house bought for £295,000: nothing on the first £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000 (£2,500) and 5% on the final £45,000 (£2,250), so **£4,750**.

On a £250,000 main home the same bands give nothing on the first £125,000 and 2% on the next £125,000, so **£2,500**.

First-time buyers pay nothing on the first £300,000 and 5% on the portion from £300,001 to £500,000. If the price is over £500,000 the relief is lost and the normal rates apply. [First-time buyer conveyancing costs](/blog/first-time-buyer-conveyancing-costs/) explains who qualifies. Buying an additional property usually adds 5% to each band. Use [HMRC's calculator](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/calculate-stamp-duty-land-tax/) for your own figure. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) sets England beside Wales and Scotland.

The return and the tax are due within 14 days of completion. Your conveyancer will usually ask for the money before then.

## How much is the Land Registry fee?

HM Land Registry charges a scale fee to register you as the owner. For a transfer of the whole of a registered title, the [Land Registration Fee Order 2024](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-registration-services-fees) (in force since 9 December 2024) sets these fees. The lower column is an electronic application. A new lease, or a transfer of only part of a title, is charged at the postal rate even when it is sent electronically.

| Price | By post | Electronic, whole of a registered title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to £80,000 | £45 | £20 |
| £80,001 to £100,000 | £95 | £40 |
| £100,001 to £200,000 | £230 | £100 |
| £200,001 to £500,000 | £330 | £150 |
| £500,001 to £1,000,000 | £655 | £295 |
| £1,000,001 and over | £1,105 | £500 |

A registered home bought for between £200,001 and £500,000 therefore costs **£150** to register if the application is electronic, or **£330** by post. If the mortgage is lodged with the transfer, or before that application is completed, HM Land Registry charges no separate fee for the mortgage. A mortgage lodged after the transfer application has been completed is charged on Scale 2, assessed on the amount secured.

Search fees are not on this scale. Each council sets its own fee for local authority enquiries, so two quotes can show different search totals for houses in different areas. Where that council's local land charges register has moved to HM Land Registry, the official search certificate is £15.

## Buying, selling and remortgaging

The buyer's bill is the one with searches, the registration fee and stamp duty. [Selling a house](/blog/selling-a-house-solicitor-fees/) has a shorter disbursement list: official copies of the register, identity checks and sending the proceeds. The seller does not pay stamp duty. A [remortgage](/blog/remortgage-legal-fees/) is simpler again, and you need a conveyancer only if you are switching lender.

## What makes the legal fee higher?

The registration fee and the tax follow the price. The legal fee follows the work. Expect a higher fee, or a separate supplement, when the property is:

- **Leasehold**, because the lease has to be read and a management pack obtained from the freeholder or managing agent
- **A new build**, with a new title and the developer's deadline
- **Unregistered**, which means reading older paper deeds rather than an online register
- **Shared ownership, or bought with a gifted deposit**, which means extra checks and another party to deal with

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does conveyancing cost in England?

It depends on the price, the property and the firm. Compare the total of the legal fee, VAT and disbursements. Stamp Duty Land Tax is extra and is the same whichever firm you instruct. On a main home the tax is £2,500 at £250,000 and, in GOV.UK's example, £4,750 at £295,000.

### Is the lowest legal fee the lowest quote?

Only if both quotes include the same disbursements and both show VAT. A low legal fee with the searches left out can cost more once the bill is complete.

### Do I pay the Land Registry fee as well as the solicitor's fee?

Yes, when you buy. It is a disbursement. For the whole of a registered title bought for between £200,001 and £500,000 it is £150 if the application is electronic, under the Land Registration Fee Order 2024.
