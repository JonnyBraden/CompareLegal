## What the best-reviewed firms have in common

Across all four cities, the same few things come up again and again in five-star reviews:

- **Help with offers, not just the paperwork.** In Scotland, clients value a solicitor who advises them on what to offer and how to compete for a property.
- **Explaining the Scottish system.** Buyers moving from elsewhere in the UK often praise firms that walked them through Home Reports, offers and missives.
- **Speed.** Some of the highest-rated firms got clients their keys within weeks of an accepted offer.
- **Clear costs.** Reviewers notice when fees are set out at the start and the final bill matches.

You can ask any firm about these things before you instruct them. Our guide to [buying a house in Scotland](/blog/buying-a-house-in-scotland-conveyancing/) explains the process and costs. ## How we chose these firms

We searched Google for conveyancing solicitors in each city and kept firms that:

- had a Google rating of **4.7 or higher**
- had at least **30 Google reviews**
- had recent reviews showing they act on residential purchases, sales or remortgages

We ranked them by rating, then by number of reviews, and listed up to three per city. Where fewer than three firms met the standard, we listed fewer rather than lowering it. Firms with several offices are rated separately on Google, so we've used the rating for the office named. Ratings and review counts were checked on 16 November 2026 and will change over time.

A Google rating covers all of a firm's work, not just conveyancing. The summaries above paraphrase the themes that come up most often in each firm's reviews. They are not a complete picture, and individual experiences vary, so read the reviews yourself and get a written quote before you decide. No firm paid to be included. ### Do I need a Scottish solicitor to buy a house in Scotland?

Yes. Scotland has its own property law, missives and land register, so you need a firm qualified to act in Scotland. A firm in England can't act on a Scottish purchase.

### Is a highly rated solicitor more expensive?

Not necessarily. A rating tells you about service, not price. Compare fixed-fee quotes that include VAT and outlays, then use reviews to choose between firms at a similar price.
