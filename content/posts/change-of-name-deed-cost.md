Changing your name by deed poll costs **£53.05** if you enrol it with the High Court in England and Wales, and there is no court fee if you make an unenrolled deed poll yourself. The fee differs in Scotland and Northern Ireland: there is no deed poll enrolment, National Records of Scotland charges **£40** where the birth or adoption was registered in Scotland, and Northern Ireland charges **£35** to record a change and **£81** for a Court of Judicature deed poll application (checked November 2026).

## Do you need a solicitor to change your name?

In England and Wales, no. From 16 you can write an unenrolled deed poll yourself, and Wales uses the same rules. In Scotland you post the National Records of Scotland form yourself. In Northern Ireland a justice of the peace, a lay magistrate or a solicitor can witness the register form. A solicitor's drafting fee is the firm's own: see [questions to ask before you instruct a solicitor](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/). You do not need a deed poll to take a spouse's surname. After divorce, some organisations ask for one as well. Those court fees are in [divorce costs across the UK](/blog/divorce-costs-uk/).

## How much does an enrolled deed poll cost in England and Wales?

GOV.UK states **£53.05** for an adult and the same **£53.05** for a child (checked November 2026). The change is published in The Gazette. You can ask the judge to limit that notice. GOV.UK lists no further Gazette fee.

Sign the deed before two witnesses and the statutory declaration before a solicitor or another authorised witness, and send it to the Deed Poll Office at the Royal Courts of Justice on the Strand, London. [Form LOC020](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/enrol-a-deed-poll-with-the-court-to-change-your-name-form-loc020) applies to England, Northern Ireland and Wales, so this £53.05 route is open from Northern Ireland as well as the Belfast fee below. Some banks accept only an enrolled deed, so ask them before you pay to enrol.

## How do you change your name in Scotland?

There is no enrolment. If the birth or adoption was registered in Scotland, National Records of Scotland updates that entry for **£40**, plus **£10** for each other family member in the same application (page updated 19 May 2026, checked November 2026). [mygov.scot](https://www.mygov.scot/change-name-public-register) links to it. Under 16 the fee is the same £40. Marriage and divorce use the certificate, not this fee. If you were born outside Scotland, use a deed poll, a statutory declaration, or the process where you were born. A justice of the peace witnesses a declaration [free of charge](https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/courts-and-tribunals/sheriff-and-justice-of-the-peace-courts/justice-of-the-peace-courts/signing-documents/) (checked November 2026).

## How does a deed poll work in Northern Ireland?

If you were born or adopted in Northern Ireland, the General Register Office records a forename or surname for **£35**, plus **£16** for another family member at the same time and **£15** for a certificate (nidirect, checked November 2026). A justice of the peace or lay magistrate witnesses the form without a charge. The Court of Judicature charges **£81** from 1 April 2026 for an application for a deed poll at the [Royal Courts of Justice, Belfast](https://www.justice-ni.gov.uk/articles/information-our-court-offices) (checked November 2026). Ask that office which papers to lodge. This is not the £53.05 London enrolment, and nidirect does not set a fee for drafting the deed.

## What do solicitors charge for a change of name deed?

There is no set fee, and this guide quotes none. Ask whether the work is an unenrolled deed, an enrolled deed, a Scottish declaration, or help with a register form, and whether the official fee and VAT are included. The same questions are in [questions to ask before you instruct a solicitor](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/).

## When does a child's change of name need extra steps?

**England and Wales.** Enrolment is **£53.05** and needs everyone with parental responsibility to agree, or a court order (checked November 2026). If the adults cannot agree, a specific issue order is **£270** in EX50, updated on 26 October 2026 (checked November 2026). The GOV.UK name page still shows £263, so use EX50. [Help with fees](https://www.gov.uk/get-help-with-court-fees) may apply. See [children arrangements order costs](/blog/children-arrangements-order-cost/).

**Scotland.** The adults with parental responsibilities and rights sign the under-16 form. If both parents have them, both sign. If one will not, National Records of Scotland needs a court order. The fee stays **£40** (checked November 2026).

**Northern Ireland.** nidirect names the qualified applicants, including both parents where both have parental responsibility. The fee is **£35** (checked November 2026).

## What should you check before you pay?

- Which official fee you are paying, in London, Scotland, or Belfast, and whether VAT is inside the quote
- For a child, that everyone who must agree has done so, or that a court order exists
- HM Passport Office accepts a deed poll (enrolled or unenrolled), a statutory declaration, an affidavit, or a Scottish certificate showing both names, plus proof you use the new name
- DVLA charges nothing to change a Great Britain licence name. Send the original. A new photo at the same time is **£17** (checked November 2026). London enrolment is published in The Gazette

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my name without a solicitor?

Yes. In England and Wales an unenrolled deed poll has no court fee from age 16, and enrolment is £53.05. Scotland's register fee is £40 if you were born or adopted there. Northern Ireland charges £35 to record a change, and a justice of the peace can witness the form free.

### How much does an enrolled deed poll cost?

In England and Wales it is £53.05 for an adult or a child. An unenrolled deed poll has no court fee.

### Does Scotland have deed poll enrolment?

Scotland records the change for £40 with National Records of Scotland, plus £10 for each other family member applying too, where the birth or adoption was registered there. People born outside Scotland use a deed poll or a statutory declaration. A justice of the peace witnesses a declaration free of charge.

### What does it cost to change a child's name?

England and Wales: £53.05 to enrol if everyone with parental responsibility agrees, or £270 for a specific issue order. Scotland: £40, signed by the adults with parental responsibilities and rights. Northern Ireland: £35 at the General Register Office.

### Will HM Passport Office accept an unenrolled deed poll?

Yes. It accepts an enrolled or unenrolled deed poll, a statutory declaration, an affidavit, or a Scottish birth certificate showing both names, with evidence you use the new name. DVLA charges nothing for the name alone on a Great Britain licence.
