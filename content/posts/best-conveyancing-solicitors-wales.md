## What should a conveyancing quote in Wales include?

A quote for a purchase in Wales should separate the firm's own charge from money paid to other people. Ask for:

- the legal fee
- VAT on that fee
- searches and reports, including the local authority search
- the HM Land Registry fee to register the purchase
- Land Transaction Tax, paid to the Welsh Revenue Authority
- extras, such as leasehold, a new build, shared ownership or a mortgage

England and Wales share a legal system. Solicitors are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, and the title is registered at HM Land Registry. Buyers in Wales pay Land Transaction Tax. Buyers in England pay Stamp Duty Land Tax.
