Buying or selling a home in Wales works much as it does in England, with one important difference: you pay Land Transaction Tax to the Welsh Revenue Authority instead of Stamp Duty. A local conveyancer who handles Welsh purchases every day will get that right. They'll also know the local searches and the firms on the other side of your chain. ## What the best-reviewed firms have in common

Across all three cities, the same few things come up again and again in five-star reviews:

- **Updates without chasing.** Clients most often praise being told what was happening before they had to ask.
- **Plain explanations.** First-time buyers in particular value a solicitor who explains each stage, from searches to completion, without jargon.
- **Persistence when things go wrong.** Many of the best reviews come from difficult purchases: slow chains, leasehold issues or a mortgage offer about to expire.
- **No surprises on cost.** Reviewers notice when the final bill matches the quote.

You can ask any firm about these things before you instruct them. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who are the best conveyancing solicitors in Cardiff?

Based on Google reviews in November 2026, the highest-rated conveyancing firms in Cardiff with at least 30 reviews were HCB Solicitors, Ackland & Co Solicitors and Martyn Prowel Gartsides. The best firm for you also depends on price, availability and the property you're buying.

### Can a solicitor in England handle my purchase in Wales?

Yes. England and Wales share a legal system, so any solicitor or licensed conveyancer in either country can act. Make sure they're used to Welsh purchases, because Land Transaction Tax has different rates and bands from Stamp Duty and is paid to the Welsh Revenue Authority.

### Is a highly rated solicitor more expensive?

Not necessarily. A rating tells you about service, not price. Compare fixed-fee quotes that include VAT and disbursements, then use reviews to choose between firms at a similar price.
