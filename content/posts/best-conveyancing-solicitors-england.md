## What the best-reviewed firms have in common

Across all four cities, the same few things come up again and again in five-star reviews:

- **Updates without chasing.** Clients most often praise being told what was happening before they had to ask, whether by phone, email or an online tracker.
- **Plain explanations.** First-time buyers in particular value a conveyancer who explains searches, enquiries and the contract without jargon.
- **Pushing the chain along.** Many of the best reviews come from difficult transactions where the firm chased other solicitors, lenders or the seller to keep things moving.
- **Being easy to reach.** Quick replies matter more to reviewers than almost anything else.

You can ask any firm about these things before you instruct them. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who are the best conveyancing solicitors in Manchester?

Based on Google reviews in November 2026, the highest-rated conveyancing firms in Manchester with at least 30 reviews were HLF Berry Solicitors, MS Law Solicitors and Prosperity Law. The best firm for you also depends on price, availability and the property you're buying.

### Who are the best conveyancing solicitors in Birmingham?

On the same basis, the highest-rated firms in Birmingham were Newhall Solicitors, Blackfriars Law Solicitors and Goldsmiths Solicitors.

### Is a solicitor better than a licensed conveyancer?

Both can handle a home purchase or sale in England. Solicitors are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, and licensed conveyancers by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. What matters most is the firm's experience with your type of property and how well it communicates.

### Is a highly rated conveyancer more expensive?

Not necessarily. A rating tells you about service, not price. Compare fixed-fee quotes that include VAT and disbursements, then use reviews to choose between firms at a similar price.
