The court fee for ending a marriage is published. What a solicitor charges is not, and most of the cost sits in the finances rather than in the divorce itself. In England and Wales the fee to apply for divorce is £628, whether you apply alone or together, and a consent order to make a financial agreement binding is a further £62. Both figures have applied since 13 July 2026. Scotland and Northern Ireland charge different court fees and use different grounds. You can [compare divorce and family law fees](/family-law/) with VAT and disbursements included. The guides on [the family law hub](/blog/category/family-law/) sit alongside this one.

## What are the court fees?

Checked 21 November 2026. These are court fees, not solicitor's fees. People on a low income may be able to get help with fees in each system.

| | What you pay the court | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England and Wales | Apply for a divorce, nullity or civil partnership dissolution | £628, from 13 July 2026 |
| England and Wales | Ask the court to approve a financial order by consent | £62 |
| England and Wales | Apply for a financial order that is not by consent | £321 |
| Scotland | Simplified divorce or dissolution, where you qualify | £156, from 1 April 2026 |
| Scotland | Initial writ in an ordinary divorce or dissolution | £191, from 1 April 2026 |
| Northern Ireland | Lodge a divorce or dissolution petition | £326, as published by the Department of Justice |

Northern Ireland also charges to list the case for hearing and to apply for the decree absolute. [nidirect](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/getting-divorce-or-dissolving-civil-partnership) publishes those further fees: £407 for a County Court hearing, £488 for a High Court hearing, and £123 for the decree absolute, when checked on 21 November 2026. An undefended case is usually in the County Court. A defended case is heard in the High Court.

## The divorce and the finances are separate

A divorce ends the marriage. It does not divide the home, savings or pensions, and it does not end financial claims. That needs a separate agreement, made binding by a court. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland that document is a consent order. In Scotland it is usually a minute of agreement, which can be registered so it can be enforced. Many people skip this step. Claims can be brought years later.

When you compare fees, check whether a quote covers:

- **the divorce only**, or
- **the divorce plus the financial agreement**.

An agreed case is often a fixed fee. Disputes about money or children are usually charged by the hour, and that is where costs rise. Mediation is often much cheaper than a contested hearing. There is no official "typical" solicitor's fee, so do not compare a court fee with a headline that leaves the consent order out.

If the home changes hands, that is a conveyancing job as well as a family-law job. A transfer between spouses as part of a divorce or separation agreement is treated differently for tax. [Transfer of equity solicitor fees](/blog/transfer-of-equity-solicitor-fees/) explains the difference. Divorce is also a reason to review a will, because marriage and divorce change what an existing will does. [Wills and powers of attorney](/blog/wills-and-powers-of-attorney-uk-differences/) explains how that differs by nation. The same checks on any quote are in [questions to ask before instructing a solicitor](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/).

## How does the process differ?

### England and Wales

Since 6 April 2022 you do not need to blame your spouse or wait out a period of separation. One of you, or both of you jointly, states that the marriage has broken down irretrievably. There is a minimum of 20 weeks between the application and the conditional order, then at least six weeks and one day before the final order. Most uncontested divorces take six to eight months. You can apply online yourself. A solicitor's fixed fee usually covers preparing the application and applying for each order. The court cannot approve a consent order before the conditional order.

### Scotland

You can divorce if the marriage has broken down irretrievably. The grounds are one year's separation with your spouse's consent, two years' separation without it, adultery, or behaviour that means you cannot reasonably be expected to live together. The simplified procedure is the cheaper do-it-yourself route if there are no children under 16, no financial claims to be settled in the divorce, and no other court case about the marriage. The court fee for that application is £156. Other cases use the ordinary procedure in the sheriff court, usually with a solicitor, and the fee to start that case is £191. Financial agreements are normally recorded in a minute of agreement.

### Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland has not adopted no-fault divorce. You cannot petition until two years after the date of the marriage, and you must show that the marriage has broken down irretrievably by one of five facts: two years' separation with consent, five years' separation, unreasonable behaviour, adultery, or desertion for two years. Most petitions rely on separation. Even an undefended case normally includes a short hearing. The Department of Justice consulted in 2025 on moving towards a no-fault system, so check the current position before you start. You can [compare family law fees in Northern Ireland](/family-law/northern-ireland/).

## What makes a divorce cost more?

- Disagreement about the finances, the family home or pensions
- Disputes about where the children will live and how they will spend time with each parent
- Complex assets, such as a business, a farm or property abroad
- A spouse who does not respond to the court papers

None of those has a fixed court fee that covers the solicitor's time. Agreeing what you can, and paying for a consent order or minute of agreement so the agreement binds you, is usually the smaller bill.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the court fee for a divorce in England and Wales?

£628 to apply, from 13 July 2026, whether you apply alone or jointly. A consent order, so a financial agreement becomes binding, is a further £62. A solicitor's fee is on top. People on a low income can apply for Help with Fees.

### What does a divorce cost in Scotland and Northern Ireland?

In Scotland, from 1 April 2026, the sheriff court fee is £156 for a simplified divorce and £191 to start an ordinary divorce. In Northern Ireland the fee to lodge a petition is £326, with further fees to list the hearing and for the decree absolute. Solicitor's fees are extra in both places.

### What is the cheapest way to get divorced?

Agree as much as you can. In England and Wales you can apply for the divorce online yourself and pay a solicitor a fixed fee to draft the consent order. In Scotland, the simplified procedure is the lower court fee if you qualify. The saving disappears if you later dispute money or children at an hourly rate.

### Is the financial agreement included in a divorce quote?

Not always. The divorce ends the marriage. Sharing the home, savings and pensions is separate: a consent order in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or a minute of agreement in Scotland. Check which of those the quote covers, and whether VAT and the court fee are included.

### Do we both need a solicitor?

Not for the application itself. One person can apply, and in England and Wales you can apply jointly. A solicitor can usually act for only one of you. If one of you has a solicitor, the other should take their own advice before signing a financial agreement.

### Can I get divorced in Northern Ireland if we married in England?

Yes, if either of you is domiciled in Northern Ireland or has lived there for at least a year before the petition. Northern Ireland's own grounds apply, including the rule that a petition cannot be brought until two years after the marriage.
