Conveyancing in Wales uses the same legal steps as England: searches, an exchange of contracts, completion, and registration at HM Land Registry. The tax is different. Buyers pay Land Transaction Tax to the Welsh Revenue Authority, not Stamp Duty Land Tax to HMRC. There is no separate first-time buyer relief. On a main home, the nil-rate band of £225,000 applies to every buyer. The solicitor's or licensed conveyancer's fee is set by the firm. You can [compare conveyancing fees in Wales](/conveyancing/wales/) with VAT and disbursements in the total.

A solicitor or licensed conveyancer qualified in England and Wales can act on a Welsh property, wherever their office is. Both are regulated, solicitors by the [Solicitors Regulation Authority](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/transparency/) and licensed conveyancers by the [Council for Licensed Conveyancers](https://www.clc-uk.org/).

## What should a Welsh quote include?

The quote has the same three parts as one for a home in England. [How to read a conveyancing quote](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/) goes through them. In short:

1. **The legal fee**, for the firm's own work. Most firms use a fixed fee that rises with the price.
2. **VAT** at 20% on that fee.
3. **Disbursements**, including searches and the HM Land Registry fee. The official figures are in [conveyancing disbursements explained](/blog/conveyancing-disbursements-explained/).

Land Transaction Tax sits alongside that bill. It is the same whichever firm you instruct.

| Part of what you pay | Who sets it |
| --- | --- |
| Legal fee and VAT | The firm, plus VAT at 20% on the fee |
| Searches | Each council and the search providers. There is no single Welsh search price |
| HM Land Registry fee | HM Land Registry, on a scale by price. Wales uses the same registry as England |
| Land Transaction Tax | The Welsh Revenue Authority, on the bands below |

Because Wales and England share HM Land Registry, the registration fee is the same scale. Under the [Land Registration Fee Order 2024](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-registration-services-fees), a transfer of the whole of a registered title for between £200,001 and £500,000 costs **£150** if the application is electronic, or **£330** by post. Checked 21 November 2026.

## How Land Transaction Tax works

Welsh Ministers set the rates. The main residential rates below have applied since 10 October 2022, and they are the rates still published by the Welsh Revenue Authority. The draft budget of 17 November 2026 says the rates and thresholds will stay the same for 2027-28. The table is from [GOV.WALES](https://www.gov.wales/land-transaction-tax-rates-and-bands), checked 21 November 2026. Each rate applies only to the slice of the price in that band.

| Part of the price | Main residential rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to and including £225,000 | 0% |
| Over £225,000 up to and including £400,000 | 6% |
| Over £400,000 up to and including £750,000 | 7.5% |
| Over £750,000 up to and including £1,500,000 | 10% |
| Over £1,500,000 | 12% |

The Welsh Government's own example is a house bought for £280,000: nothing on the first £225,000 and 6% on the remaining £55,000, so **£3,300**.

On a £300,000 main home the same bands give nothing on £225,000 and 6% on £75,000, so **£4,500**. A buyer in England pays Stamp Duty Land Tax of £5,000 on the same price, or nothing if they qualify for first-time buyer relief. Wales has no equivalent relief, so a first-time buyer pays the £4,500 as well. At higher prices the Welsh 6% and 7.5% bands can come out higher than England. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) puts the three taxes side by side, and [conveyancing costs in England](/blog/conveyancing-costs-england/) shows the English calculation.

Use the [Welsh Revenue Authority calculator](https://www.gov.wales/land-transaction-tax-calculator) for a figure of your own. The return is due within 30 days of completion, and your conveyancer will usually file it.

### Higher rates if you already own a home

An additional residential property, including most company purchases, uses a separate set of higher rates rather than a simple surcharge. Those rates have applied since 11 December 2024, and they start at 5% on the first £180,000. The Welsh Government's example of a second home at £260,000 is **£15,950**: 5% on £180,000 (£9,000), 8.5% on the next £70,000 (£5,950) and 10% on the final £10,000 (£1,000). Replacing your main home can bring you back to the main rates. The full higher-rate table is on [GOV.WALES](https://www.gov.wales/land-transaction-tax-rates-and-bands).

## What pushes the legal fee up

The tax and the Land Registry fee follow the price and the bands above. The legal fee follows the work. Leasehold homes, which are common in parts of Cardiff and the other cities, need the lease read and a management pack from the freeholder or managing agent. New builds, unregistered titles, shared ownership and a gifted deposit add paperwork too. Ask the firm what would move a fixed fee, and what you would pay if the purchase fell through.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does conveyancing cost in Wales?

The firm's fee is its own price, plus VAT and disbursements. Land Transaction Tax is extra. On a main home there is nothing to pay on the first £225,000. The Welsh Government's example of a £280,000 purchase is £3,300, and a £300,000 main home comes to £4,500 on the published bands.

### Do first-time buyers pay Land Transaction Tax?

There is no first-time buyer relief in Wales. The £225,000 nil-rate band already applies to every buyer of a main home, so many first homes pay no Land Transaction Tax, and a first-time buyer of a dearer home pays the same as anyone else.

### Is the solicitor's work different from England?

The conveyancing steps match England, and the same HM Land Registry scale applies. The difference in the bill is the tax, which the Welsh Revenue Authority collects, and the fact that first-time buyer relief is an England and Northern Ireland rule.
