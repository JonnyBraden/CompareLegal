Disbursements are the costs your conveyancer pays to other people on your behalf. In Scotland and Northern Ireland they are often called outlays. They are not the firm's legal fee, and they are not the VAT on that fee. A quote that leaves them out is not a total you can compare. Many of them are the same whichever firm you instruct, because a public body sets the price. The legal fee is the part that differs. You can [compare conveyancing fees](/conveyancing/) with VAT and the disbursements each firm has listed.

[How to read a conveyancing quote](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/) shows where they sit in the bill. The fees below were checked on 21 November 2026.

## Which costs are disbursements?

| Item | Who usually pays | Who sets the price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local authority searches | Buyer | Each council, so there is no national price |
| Local land charges official search, where HM Land Registry holds that register | Buyer | HM Land Registry: £15 |
| Drainage, environmental and similar reports | Buyer | The water company or the report provider |
| Land registration | Buyer | The registry for that nation, on a scale by price |
| Official copies of the register | Seller, and sometimes the buyer | The registry |
| Priority search and bankruptcy search | Buyer, in England and Wales | HM Land Registry, per search or per name |
| Identity checks and a bank transfer | Buyer and seller | The firm. There is no official tariff |
| Stamp duty, Land Transaction Tax or LBTT | Buyer | The tax authority. This is a tax, not a disbursement |

## Searches when you buy in England or Wales

A buyer's conveyancer orders searches. The seller's conveyancer does not, which is why a sale quote looks thinner. [Selling a house: solicitor fees](/blog/selling-a-house-solicitor-fees/) covers that side.

- **Local authority enquiries**, often called a CON29, ask the council about planning, roads, notices and similar matters. Each council sets its own fee. There is no official national price.
- **Local land charges.** Planning agreements, tree orders, conservation areas and financial charges can be local land charges. Where that register has moved to HM Land Registry, an [official search certificate costs £15](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-local-land-charges-fees). A repeat official search of the same property within six months is free. Until a council's register has transferred, you still search it through the council, at the council's fee.
- **Drainage and water, environmental, and mining reports** are sold by the water company or by a search provider. A lender may insist on a mining search in former coalfield areas. The provider sets the price, so it belongs on the quote as its own line.

## HM Land Registry fees in England and Wales

The fee to register a purchase of the whole of a registered title is Scale 1 of the [Land Registration Fee Order 2024](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-registration-services-fees), in force since 9 December 2024. Electronic applications for the whole of a title use the lower column. A new lease, or a transfer of only part of a title, is charged at the postal rate even when it is sent electronically.

| Price | By post | Electronic, whole of a registered title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to £80,000 | £45 | £20 |
| £80,001 to £100,000 | £95 | £40 |
| £100,001 to £200,000 | £230 | £100 |
| £200,001 to £500,000 | £330 | £150 |
| £500,001 to £1,000,000 | £655 | £295 |
| £1,000,001 and over | £1,105 | £500 |

A registered home bought for £250,000 therefore costs **£150** to register electronically, because £250,000 falls in the £200,001 to £500,000 band. [Conveyancing costs in England](/blog/conveyancing-costs-england/) and [conveyancing costs in Wales](/blog/conveyancing-costs-wales/) use this same scale. Wales shares HM Land Registry with England.

If the mortgage is lodged with the transfer, or before that application is completed, there is no separate Land Registry fee for the mortgage. A mortgage lodged afterwards is charged on Scale 2, on the amount secured. Scale 2 is also the scale for a [remortgage](/blog/remortgage-legal-fees/). HM Land Registry publishes the Scale 2 table on the same fees page.

Other HM Land Registry fees on a normal purchase or sale come from the [information services fees](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-information-services-fees) and the [Land Charges fees](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-land-charges-fees), both under rules that took effect on 9 December 2024.

| Application | Electronic | By post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official copy of the register, or of the title plan | £7 each | £11 each |
| Official search with priority (OS1) on the whole title | £7 | £11 |
| Bankruptcy search (form K16), per name | £6 | £7 |

A sale usually needs an official copy of the register and of the plan, so two electronic copies are £7 plus £7. The bankruptcy search is charged for each buyer when there is a mortgage.

## Registration fees in Scotland

Registers of Scotland charges for registering the disposition, the deed that transfers ownership. The fee is based on the price in the deed or the value of the property, whichever is greater. The bands below are from the [Registers of Scotland fee table](https://kb.ros.gov.uk/fees/registration-fees), last updated 7 July 2026. Voluntary registration, where no sale is forcing the title onto the register, uses a lower column on that page.

| Price or value | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £50,000 | £80 |
| £50,001 to £100,000 | £140 |
| £100,001 to £150,000 | £260 |
| £150,001 to £200,000 | £400 |
| £200,001 to £300,000 | £530 |
| £300,001 to £500,000 | £660 |
| £500,001 to £700,000 | £800 |
| £700,001 to £1,000,000 | £930 |

A £250,000 purchase is in the £200,001 to £300,000 band, so the registration fee is **£530**. The full table is on the Registers of Scotland page. Property enquiry certificates from Scottish councils are the local searches, and each council sets its own fee. [Buying a house in Scotland](/blog/buying-a-house-in-scotland-conveyancing/) explains the rest of the bill, including Land and Buildings Transaction Tax.

## Registration fees in Northern Ireland

Land Registry NI, part of Land & Property Services, charges under the [Land Registry (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2014/139). These are the fees in that order for a transfer, checked 21 November 2026. Electronic lodgement is the lower column.

| Value | Standard fee | Electronic lodgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| £20,000 or less | £100 | £80 |
| £20,001 to £80,000 | £160 | £120 |
| £80,001 to £100,000 | £220 | £160 |
| £100,001 to £150,000 | £260 | £220 |
| £150,001 to £200,000 | £370 | £310 |
| £200,001 to £250,000 | £425 | £355 |
| £250,001 and over | £535 | £445 |

A transfer at £250,000 is in the £200,001 to £250,000 band, so the electronic fee is **£355**. Searches in Northern Ireland include property certificates and a search in the Enforcement of Judgments Office. Those are outlays and should be itemised. The fees are not on this scale. [Conveyancing costs in Northern Ireland](/blog/conveyancing-costs-northern-ireland/) explains when an unregistered title, still recorded in the Registry of Deeds, adds work.

## What is not a disbursement

The legal fee, and the VAT on it, are the firm's charges. Stamp Duty Land Tax, Land Transaction Tax and Land and Buildings Transaction Tax are taxes. Your conveyancer collects them, but the amount does not depend on which firm you choose. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) has the bands.

Identity checks and a telegraphic transfer fee are real, and firms do charge them, but there is no official price. If one quote includes them and another says "plus disbursements" without listing them, you are not comparing the same total.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are conveyancing disbursements?

They are payments your conveyancer makes to other people for you, such as searches and land-registration fees. They are listed on top of the legal fee and VAT. In Scotland and Northern Ireland the usual word is outlays.

### How much does a local authority search cost?

There is no national price. Each council sets the fee for its own enquiries. Where HM Land Registry holds that area's local land charges register, the official search certificate is £15, and the council's other enquiries are still charged separately.

### How much is the Land Registry fee when I buy?

It depends on the price and the nation. In England and Wales, an electronic application to register the whole of a title bought for between £200,001 and £500,000 is £150. In Scotland a £250,000 disposition costs £530 to register. In Northern Ireland a £250,000 transfer lodged electronically costs £355.

### Are disbursements the same whichever firm I use?

The registry fees are. Search fees follow the council and the reports your lender wants, not a discount from the firm. Identity checks and the bank transfer fee are the firm's own charges, so those lines can differ. Compare the total.
