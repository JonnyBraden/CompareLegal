The official cost of a power of attorney is the registration fee, and each part of the UK charges its own. A solicitor's fee for advice and drafting sits on top, and is set by the firm. You can [compare power of attorney fees](/wills/) with VAT included. The fees below were checked on 21 November 2026.

| | Document | When the official fee is paid | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| England and Wales | Lasting power of attorney (LPA): property and financial affairs, and health and welfare | When you apply to the Office of the Public Guardian, before the LPA can be used | £92 each, so £184 for both |
| Scotland | Continuing power, welfare power, or both in one document | When you submit it to the Office of the Public Guardian (Scotland) | £99 per document, from 1 April 2026 |
| Northern Ireland | Enduring power of attorney (EPA), for property and finances | When the attorney applies to the Office of Care and Protection | £189, from 1 April 2026 |

[Wills and powers of attorney across the UK](/blog/wills-and-powers-of-attorney-uk-differences/) explains what each document can do. Making a will is a separate job, covered in [how much a will costs](/blog/will-cost-uk/).

## England and Wales: lasting powers of attorney

There are two LPAs. One covers property and financial affairs. The other covers health and welfare. Each is registered on its own. There is no combined discount: both together are £92 plus £92.

You can make the forms yourself with the GOV.UK online service, print them, and send them to the Office of the Public Guardian with the fee. Registration takes 8 to 10 weeks if the forms are complete. An LPA cannot be used until it is registered. A property and financial affairs LPA can then be used while you still have capacity, if you have said so. A health and welfare LPA is used when you lack capacity to make the decision in question.

If your income before tax is under £12,000 a year, you pay half, which is £46 per LPA. If you receive certain means-tested benefits, such as Income Support, you can apply to pay nothing. Universal Credit is assessed for the half-fee reduction rather than a full exemption. Use form LPA120, which the Office of the Public Guardian updated on 2 November 2026. The fee is paid with the application and is not refunded later.

If a mistake means you have to apply again, a repeat application within three months is £46.

A solicitor's fee is for taking instructions, preparing the LPA and checking it before it goes to the Office of the Public Guardian. Ask whether the quote is for one LPA or both, and whether the £92 registration fee is included or listed as a disbursement. You can [compare fees in England](/wills/england/) and [in Wales](/wills/wales/). The forms are available in Welsh.

## Scotland: continuing and welfare powers

Scotland uses a continuing power of attorney for money and property, and a welfare power of attorney for personal and medical decisions. They are often put in one document. The Office of the Public Guardian (Scotland) charges **£99** to register that document, whether it is continuing only, welfare only, or both together. Two separate documents are two fees. A deed of amendment is also £99. These fees come from the Adults with Incapacity (Public Guardian's Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2026, in force from 1 April 2026. A further table applies from 1 April 2027.

A solicitor, or a medical practitioner, must sign a certificate that you understand what you are signing. That professional's charge is separate from the £99. You may get help with a solicitor's fee through the advice and assistance scheme. The Scottish Legal Aid Board publishes who qualifies.

You may pay no registration fee if you receive certain benefits. For Personal Independence Payment or Adult Disability Payment, that exemption applies only if your gross annual income is £26,437 or less. The Office of the Public Guardian (Scotland) lists the other qualifying benefits and the exemption form. If you claim the exemption, you post the power of attorney rather than using the electronic service. You can [compare power of attorney fees in Scotland](/wills/scotland/).

## Northern Ireland: enduring powers of attorney

The usual document is an enduring power of attorney. It covers property and financial affairs. Health and welfare decisions sit outside it. The Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 provides for a lasting power of attorney. Check whether those provisions are in force before you rely on them. The Office of Care and Protection can give general guidance, and a solicitor can advise.

An EPA can be used, while you still have capacity, from the time it is signed. You do not pay a registration fee at that point. If your attorney later believes you are losing the capacity to manage your affairs, they must notify you and your relatives and apply to register the EPA with the Office of Care and Protection. The fee for that application (form EP2) is **£189** from 1 April 2026, under the Court of Judicature Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026. The Department of Justice publishes the same figure. The order sets a further increase from 1 April 2027.

People on a low income or certain benefits can apply for a court fee exemption or remission. The Department of Justice explains how. You can [compare enduring power of attorney fees in Northern Ireland](/wills/northern-ireland/).

## What to check in a solicitor's quote

- Whether the price is for one document or for both types
- Whether the official registration fee is included or extra
- In Scotland, whether the quote includes the certificate of understanding
- Whether VAT is included
- What you pay if you change your mind before registration
- Who will take your instructions, and how they will check that you understand the document

Get those answers in the client care letter. The same list is in [questions to ask a solicitor](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I pay twice in England and Wales?

You pay £92 for each LPA you register. Property and financial affairs, plus health and welfare, is £184. In Scotland, continuing and welfare powers in a single document are one registration of £99.

### Can I make a power of attorney without a solicitor?

In England and Wales, yes. The GOV.UK service produces the forms, and you pay the Office of the Public Guardian £92 per LPA. In Scotland a solicitor or a doctor must still sign the certificate of understanding. In Northern Ireland a solicitor is the usual route, and the Office of Care and Protection can explain the registration step. A mistake on the form can delay registration or mean a further fee.

### When is the Northern Ireland fee paid?

When the attorney applies to register, because they believe you can no longer manage your property and finances. Signing the enduring power of attorney does not itself attract the £189 fee.

### Is there a health and welfare power of attorney in Northern Ireland?

An enduring power of attorney covers property and financial affairs. Decisions about care and medical treatment are outside that document. The 2016 Act allows for lasting powers of attorney. Check the current commencement position before you rely on that route.
