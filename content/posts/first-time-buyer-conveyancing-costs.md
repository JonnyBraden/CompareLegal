A first home does not come with a legal fee set by law, and it does not come with a discount on searches or land registration. Firms set their own fees. What can be lower is the property tax, and only in some nations. In England and Northern Ireland, first-time buyers pay no Stamp Duty Land Tax on the first £300,000. Wales has no first-time buyer relief at all. Scotland raises the nil-rate band, and the saving is capped at £600. You can [compare conveyancing fees](/conveyancing/) on the total, including VAT and disbursements, in the same way as any other buyer.

## What a first purchase quote should include

The quote has the same three parts as any purchase. [Conveyancing disbursements explained](/blog/conveyancing-disbursements-explained/) lists the official search and registration fees. Nothing in those scales is cheaper because you have not owned a home before.

| Ask the firm to show | Why it matters on a first purchase |
| --- | --- |
| Legal fee, and VAT at 20% | This is the part that differs between firms. Tax relief does not reduce it |
| Searches | You are buying, so the search pack is yours to pay for. Councils set their own search fees |
| Land registration | A scale fee based on the price, in every nation |
| Identity checks, per person | Required for every buyer. A gifted deposit means extra questions about where the money came from |
| Bank transfer on completion | A firm charge, with no official tariff |
| Mortgage or Lifetime ISA work | Ask whether acting for your lender, or claiming a Lifetime ISA, is inside the fixed fee |
| The tax return | Your conveyancer files it and claims any relief. Confirm you qualify before you exchange |

[Questions to ask before you instruct](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/) covers fixed fees and what happens if the purchase falls through. [How long conveyancing takes](/blog/how-long-does-conveyancing-take/) covers the timetable. A first purchase uses the same stages as any other.

## The tax, nation by nation

Every buyer on the application has to qualify, and the home has to be your main residence. If one of you has owned a home before, the relief is not available. The figures below are the published bands, checked 21 November 2026. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) has a wider set of examples.

| Nation | Tax | First-time buyer rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England and Northern Ireland | Stamp Duty Land Tax, paid to HMRC | Nothing on the first £300,000, then 5% up to £500,000. Over £500,000 the relief is lost completely |
| Wales | Land Transaction Tax, paid to the Welsh Revenue Authority | No separate relief. No tax on a main home up to £225,000, for every buyer |
| Scotland | Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, paid to Revenue Scotland | The nil-rate band rises from £145,000 to £175,000. The saving is up to £600, with no price cap |

### England and Northern Ireland

[GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates) says a first-time buyer pays no Stamp Duty Land Tax up to £300,000, and 5% on the portion from £300,001 to £500,000. Its example of a £500,000 purchase is **£10,000**: nothing on £300,000 and 5% on the remaining £200,000. If the price is over £500,000 you pay the normal rates on the whole price, as if the relief did not exist.

On a £300,000 purchase the relief brings the tax to **nothing**. Without it, the ordinary bands are nothing on £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000 (£2,500) and 5% on the final £50,000 (£2,500), so **£5,000**. Northern Ireland uses these same rates. [Conveyancing costs in England](/blog/conveyancing-costs-england/) and [conveyancing costs in Northern Ireland](/blog/conveyancing-costs-northern-ireland/) show the ordinary bands. The return is due within 14 days of completion, and your conveyancer claims the relief on it. HMRC's guidance is that a company cannot claim, and that shared ownership can qualify when you will live there and the market value is £500,000 or less.

### Wales

There is no first-time buyer relief. The main residential rates already charge nothing on the first £225,000 of a main home, whoever is buying. On a £300,000 purchase that is nothing on £225,000 and 6% on £75,000, so **£4,500**, first-time buyer or not. [Conveyancing costs in Wales](/blog/conveyancing-costs-wales/) works through Land Transaction Tax, including the higher rates if you already own a home. Those higher rates are not a first-time buyer issue, but they matter if you are buying with someone who already owns.

### Scotland

[Revenue Scotland](https://revenue.scot/taxes/land-buildings-transaction-tax/residential-property) raises the nil-rate band from £145,000 to £175,000 for a first-time buyer who will live in the home. The Scottish Budget for 2026-27 keeps that relief. The extra £30,000 of nil-rate band would otherwise be taxed at 2%, so the most you can save is **£600**. There is no upper price limit of the English kind.

On a £300,000 purchase the relief gives nothing on £175,000, 2% on the next £75,000 (£1,500) and 5% on the final £50,000 (£2,500), so **£4,000**. Without the relief the same price is £4,600, because the 2% band starts at £145,000. [Buying a house in Scotland](/blog/buying-a-house-in-scotland-conveyancing/) explains missives and the Home Report, which you need to understand before you worry about the tax. Your solicitor must be qualified in Scots law.

## A Lifetime ISA is not a discount on the fee

If you are using a [Lifetime ISA](https://www.gov.uk/lifetime-isa), the provider pays the money to your conveyancer. A withdrawal towards a first home is free of the withdrawal charge when the price is £450,000 or less, you are buying with a mortgage, you will live there, and it is at least 12 months since your first payment in. From 29 October 2026, using the account on a home over £450,000 attracts a 20% withdrawal charge. That rule is about your savings, not about the solicitor's fee. Ask whether requesting the funds is included in the quote. If you also have a Help to Buy ISA, you can take the government bonus from only one of the two.

A gift from family is common on a first purchase. The conveyancer has to check where the money came from. Ask whether that work is inside the fixed fee before you treat two quotes as equal.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are solicitor fees cheaper for first-time buyers?

Not by any legal rule. The legal fee is the firm's price. Searches and land-registration fees follow the same scales as any other purchase at that price. Compare the total, including VAT and disbursements.

### Do first-time buyers pay stamp duty in England?

Nothing on the first £300,000, then 5% up to £500,000. GOV.UK's example at £500,000 is £10,000. If the price is over £500,000 the relief disappears and the ordinary rates apply. Northern Ireland uses the same rules. Everyone buying with you must be a first-time buyer.

### Do first-time buyers pay tax in Wales?

There is no first-time buyer relief. No Land Transaction Tax is due on a main home up to £225,000, for any buyer. A £300,000 purchase is £4,500 on the published bands.

### Do I still pay for searches as a first-time buyer?

Yes. The search pack and the land-registration fee are part of buying, and the official fees do not have a first-time buyer rate.
