A conveyancing quote in England or Wales is complete only when you can see five things: the legal fee, VAT, the disbursements, the property tax, and any extras that apply to the property. Two quotes for the same house can differ by hundreds of pounds because one of those lines is missing. Compare the total. You can [compare conveyancing fees in England](/conveyancing/england/) and [in Wales](/conveyancing/wales/) with VAT and disbursements included. What the work costs on an English purchase, as distinct from how to read the quote, is covered in [conveyancing costs in England](/blog/conveyancing-costs-england/).

## What should a conveyancing quote include?

| Part | What it covers | Is it the same for every firm? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal fee | The firm's work: title, enquiries, the report to you and your lender, exchange, completion and registration. Usually a fixed fee banded by price. | No. This is the figure to compare, once everything else is listed. |
| VAT | 20% of the legal fee. Some firms show prices without it. | The rate is fixed. The amount follows the fee. |
| Disbursements | Searches, HM Land Registry fees, official copies, identity checks and a bank transfer fee. | Third-party costs. They should be itemised, not hidden inside a low legal fee. |
| Stamp duty | Stamp Duty Land Tax in England, Land Transaction Tax in Wales. Your conveyancer files the return and pays the tax from your funds. | Yes. The tax does not change with the firm. |
| Extras | Leasehold, a new build, shared ownership, a gifted deposit, unregistered land, or a company or trust purchase. | Only if they apply to this property. A good quote lists them as supplements. |

Official amounts behind the disbursements and the tax were checked on 21 November 2026. Since December 2018, firms regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority must publish their residential conveyancing prices, including what is and is not covered. Licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers have similar rules. Publishing the price does not remove the need to read it.

## Which disbursements should be on a purchase quote?

- **Searches.** A local authority search, a drainage and water search and an environmental search are standard. A lender may insist on others, such as a mining search in former coalfield areas. Councils set their own search fees, so there is no national price. The quote should name each search.
- **HM Land Registry fee** to register you as owner. For a transfer of the whole of a registered title lodged electronically, the [Land Registration Fee Order 2024](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-registration-services-fees) (in force since 9 December 2024) charges £20 up to £80,000, £40 from £80,001 to £100,000, £100 from £100,001 to £200,000, £150 from £200,001 to £500,000, £295 from £500,001 to £1 million, and £500 above that. A lease, or a postal application, uses the higher column: £330, for example, where the value is £200,001 to £500,000. A mortgage registered with the purchase is not usually charged a separate fee.
- **Official copies and a pre-completion search** at HM Land Registry, plus a bankruptcy search against each buyer if there is a mortgage.
- **Identity and anti-money-laundering checks**, charged per person.
- **A bank transfer fee** for sending the money on completion.

The tax is not a disbursement and not a legal cost. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) sets the bands side by side. England and Northern Ireland share SDLT. Wales charges Land Transaction Tax, so an English quote cannot be reused for a Welsh purchase.

## Which extras push the fee up?

| Situation | Why the legal fee rises |
| --- | --- |
| Leasehold flat or house | Reviewing the lease, a management pack from the freeholder or managing agent, and notices after completion. The landlord's own fees can be large. Extending the lease is a separate job: see [lease extension solicitor fees](/blog/lease-extension-solicitor-fees/). |
| New build | A developer deadline, a new title, and checks on planning, roads and the warranty. |
| Shared ownership or a gifted deposit | A landlord as well as a seller, and checks on the source of funds. See [shared ownership and new-build costs](/blog/shared-ownership-and-new-build-conveyancing-costs/). |
| Transfer of equity | Someone is added to or removed from the title. See [transfer of equity solicitor fees](/blog/transfer-of-equity-solicitor-fees/). |
| Unregistered land | Older paper deeds, rather than an online register. |
| A company or a trust | Additional due diligence. |

## Why do fees differ between firms?

Fees reflect overheads, how much of the work a qualified lawyer does, and how many cases each person handles. Firms in central London and the South East often charge more, but any solicitor or licensed conveyancer in England and Wales can act on a property in either country, so a local office is not required. A local firm can offer a meeting and local knowledge, for example flood risk or the usual leasehold arrangements on a particular development.

A very low fee can mean a high caseload and slow replies. Look at what is included, who will handle the file, and how the firm communicates. The longer checklist is in [questions to ask before instructing a solicitor](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/). Northern Ireland is a different legal system: [conveyancing costs in Northern Ireland](/blog/conveyancing-costs-northern-ireland/) covers it. A remortgage is simpler than a purchase, and you only need a conveyancer if you are switching lender: [remortgage legal fees](/blog/remortgage-legal-fees/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does conveyancing cost in England and Wales?

There is no single fee. It depends on the price, the type of property and the firm. Compare the total of the legal fee, VAT and disbursements. Stamp Duty Land Tax or Land Transaction Tax is extra and is the same whichever firm you use.

### Is stamp duty part of the legal fee?

No. Your conveyancer usually pays it for you, but it is a tax. It should be shown separately from the legal fee and from disbursements such as searches and the Land Registry fee.

### Do I need a solicitor or a licensed conveyancer?

Either can handle a residential purchase or sale in England and Wales. Both are regulated and insured. A solicitor can also advise on wider issues, such as a trust over the property.

### Can I use a conveyancer in another city?

Yes. The work is mostly done online and by post, so the firm can be based anywhere in England or Wales. It must be qualified in England and Wales. A Scottish or Northern Ireland firm cannot act unless it is also qualified here.

### Are searches included in the legal fee?

Sometimes, and sometimes they are listed as disbursements. Ask for each search by name. A quote that leaves them out is not complete, and councils do not charge a national fee.

### Is there a charge if the purchase falls through?

There can be. Ask before you instruct. Some firms charge for work done up to that point, and search fees already paid are not usually refunded.
