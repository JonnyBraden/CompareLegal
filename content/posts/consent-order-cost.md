A financial consent order is the document that makes an agreement about money and property binding at the end of a marriage or civil partnership. In England and Wales the court fee for that application, when you apply by consent, is **£62**. The fee has applied since 13 July 2026. It is separate from the divorce application and from whatever a solicitor charges to draft the order and the statement of information that goes with it. The figures in this guide were checked on 21 November 2026.

You can [compare family law fees](/family-law/), including a financial consent order in England and Wales and a minute of agreement in Scotland. [Divorce costs across the UK](/blog/divorce-costs-uk/) covers the application that ends the marriage itself.

## The divorce and the finances are separate

A divorce ends the marriage. It leaves the financial claims you have against each other in place until an order, or a Scottish minute of agreement, deals with them. Those claims can be brought years later, which is why an agreement that can be enforced matters.

The order can cover the home, savings, investments, pensions and maintenance. Many couples ask for a clean break, so that neither can claim against the other afterwards. Where there are children, the court looks at whether the proposed order meets their needs. Arrangements for where children live, and how they spend time with each parent, are a different application. [Children arrangements](/blog/children-arrangements-order-cost/) explains that fee.

## England and Wales

HM Courts and Tribunals Service publishes the fees in guide EX50, updated on 26 October 2026. They apply in England and Wales.

| Application | Court fee from 13 July 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Financial order by consent | £62 |
| Financial order, other than by consent | £321 |
| Divorce or dissolution application, for comparison | £628 |

The £62 fee is what you pay the court to consider an agreed financial order. You send a draft order and a statement of information about each person's finances. The form for that statement is Form D81. A judge reads the papers. You do not usually attend a hearing. The court can ask questions, or refuse to make the order, if the terms look unfair.

If you have not agreed, the fee to start a financial application is £321, and the work from there is usually charged by the solicitor's hour. Hearings, disclosure and a dispute about a pension or a business are what push that bill up. People on a low income or certain benefits can apply for [Help with Fees](https://www.gov.uk/get-help-with-court-fees).

A solicitor can act for only one of you. The other person should take their own advice before signing. Ask the quote to say whether it includes:

- Drafting the order and the statement of information
- One round of changes after the other person has taken advice
- The £62 court fee, as a disbursement
- Pensions, if either of you has one
- What you pay if the other person does not sign, or the court raises a query
- VAT

Pension sharing needs an extra annex to the order. The pension scheme may charge to carry the sharing out. That charge comes from the scheme, so ask the scheme as well as the solicitor. If the quote assumes the finances are already agreed, ask what happens to the fee if they are not. [Questions to ask before you instruct](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/) is a short checklist for that conversation.

## Scotland: a minute of agreement

Scotland records a financial agreement in a **minute of agreement**, which is a contract between the two of you. It can be signed without a court case. To make it enforceable, solicitors register it in the Books of Council and Session, which is the Register of Deeds kept by Registers of Scotland.

Registration costs **£20**, including the first extract. Registers of Scotland updated that fee on 13 November 2026. If the agreement contains a clause consenting to registration for preservation and execution, the extract can be used as the basis for enforcing it. A digital extract and a paper extract are each charged if you ask for both. The solicitor's fee for negotiating and drafting the minute is separate, and is the main part of the cost when the finances take time to agree.

If you cannot agree, and the dispute goes into a sheriff court divorce, the fee for an initial writ in an ordinary divorce (anything other than the simplified procedure) is **£191** from 1 April 2026. That is the fee to start the case. Further court fees apply as it goes on, and solicitors usually charge by the hour. You can [compare family law fees in Scotland](/family-law/scotland/). The simplified divorce procedure is only available where there are no children under 16 and no outstanding financial claims, so a couple with finances still to resolve will not be on that route.

## Northern Ireland

nidirect lists **£407 in the county court** and **£488 in the High Court** as the fee for a court date, on top of £326 to lodge the petition and £123 for the decree absolute. The Family Proceedings Fees Order, from 1 April 2026, uses those same amounts for a notice of application for ancillary relief, which is how financial claims are put before the court. An application for an order by consent is treated differently from other applications in the case. If you have already agreed the terms, ask the court office or your solicitor which fee applies to the form you are lodging. The solicitor's work to draft the order is on top.

Northern Ireland has its own divorce grounds, including a two-year bar from the date of the marriage. [Divorce costs](/blog/divorce-costs-uk/) sets those out. You can [compare family law fees in Northern Ireland](/family-law/northern-ireland/). The Department of Justice consulted on a no-fault system in 2025, so check the current position before you start.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a consent order included in the divorce fee?

In England and Wales, no. The divorce application is £628 and the consent application for a financial order is a further £62. A solicitor's fee for drafting is extra again. Check the quote to see which of those it covers.

### What if we already agree?

The court fee in England and Wales is the £62 consent fee, and many solicitors will draft and lodge the order for a fixed fee. Each of you should take your own advice before signing. The court still has to approve the terms.

### Does a consent order cover pensions?

Only the pensions the order mentions. Sharing a pension needs a pension sharing annex, and the scheme may charge to implement it. Ask whether the quote includes pensions before you compare it with a quote that leaves them out.

### What do we use in Scotland?

A minute of agreement. Registering it in the Books of Council and Session costs £20, including the first extract. That registration fee is separate from the solicitor's fee for writing the agreement.
