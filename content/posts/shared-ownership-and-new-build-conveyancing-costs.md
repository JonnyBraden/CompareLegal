Shared-ownership and new-build quotes are higher because there is more to check, not because the law adds a surcharge to the legal fee. A shared-ownership purchase is usually a lease from a landlord, with a mortgage on the share you buy and a choice about how property tax is paid. A new build adds the developer's contract, a deadline and a title that does not exist yet. In both cases, compare the total of the legal fee, VAT and disbursements. The tax is the same whichever firm you use. [Compare conveyancing fees](/conveyancing/) on that basis. How to read any quote is in [conveyancing quotes in England and Wales](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/).

## What is in the quote?

Checked 21 November 2026. The legal fee is set by the firm. The lines below are why it is often higher than a standard freehold purchase, and which official costs sit beside it.

| Part | Shared ownership | New build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal fee | The lease, the landlord's requirements, the mortgage offer and the tax choice. | The developer contract, the deadline, planning, roads and the new title. |
| VAT | 20% of the legal fee. | 20% of the legal fee. |
| Landlord or developer costs | The landlord may charge for the management pack, notices and its own solicitor. Ask for each figure. There is no national tariff. | You instruct your own conveyancer. Any contribution the developer offers should be written into the contract. |
| Land registry | A new lease does not get the reduced electronic HM Land Registry fee. Scale 1 on the price, higher column. | A first registration or a new lease uses Scale 1. The reduced electronic fee is for a transfer of a title that is already registered. |
| Property tax | SDLT in England and Northern Ireland, with a choice of paying on the share or on the market value. LTT in Wales has its own election. LBTT in Scotland. | The normal tax on the price you pay. No special new-build rate. |
| Searches and identity checks | As on any purchase, and they should be itemised. | As on any purchase. A new site can need extra searches, such as planning or mining. |

The HM Land Registry bands are in the [registration services fees](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hm-land-registry-registration-services-fees) under the Land Registration Fee Order 2024, in force since 9 December 2024. For a lease, or any application that does not get the electronic reduction, the fee in the £200,001 to £500,000 band is £330. A transfer of the whole of a title that is already registered, lodged electronically, is £150 in that band. The quote should say which column it has used.

## Why is shared-ownership conveyancing more work?

You buy a share, and pay rent to the landlord on the rest. The landlord is usually a housing association or a local authority. The conveyancer reads the lease, including restrictions on selling and how staircasing works, reports to a lender who will only lend on those terms, deals with the landlord's notices, and explains the tax choice before exchange. One of the tax options cannot be undone.

Staircasing, where you buy a further share later, is a separate transaction. It is not included in the purchase fee unless the quote says so. Selling later also involves the landlord, so that quote is higher than a standard sale for the same reason. A higher purchase quote means the file has more parties. It is not a judgment on the scheme.

## How does the tax choice work?

### England and Northern Ireland

[HMRC's shared ownership guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sdlt-shared-ownership-property) gives two ways to pay Stamp Duty Land Tax when a qualifying body grants the lease. A qualifying body includes a housing association, a local housing authority and the Northern Ireland Housing Executive.

- **A market value election.** You pay SDLT once, on the market value stated in the lease, as if you had bought the home outright. You pay nothing more on later staircasing, or on buying the freehold if the lease allows it. The election is made in the SDLT return, or by amending it within the deadline, and you cannot cancel it. HMRC's example is a home with a market value of £140,000 where you buy a 50% share for £70,000: SDLT is worked out on £140,000, which is nothing on £125,000 and 2% on £15,000, so £300. That matches the main-home rates in force since 1 April 2025.
- **Pay in stages.** You pay any SDLT due on the premium for the first share. You pay no more until your share goes above 80%.

Which is better depends on the market value, whether you expect to staircase, and whether first-time buyer relief applies. The conveyancer should set both figures out before you exchange. The bands for an ordinary purchase are in [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/).

In Northern Ireland, registration is at Land Registry NI, not HM Land Registry, and the solicitor must be qualified there. [Conveyancing costs in Northern Ireland](/blog/conveyancing-costs-northern-ireland/) has that scale. Co-Ownership buyers should ask whether HMRC's election is available on their particular lease, rather than assume it.

### Wales

You pay Land Transaction Tax, not SDLT. For a qualifying shared-ownership lease you can elect for LTT to be charged on the market value stated in the lease, and for rent not to be counted. The election is made in the return and cannot be withdrawn. The Welsh Revenue Authority's [technical guidance on social housing](https://www.gov.wales/land-transaction-tax-relief-certain-transactions-relating-social-housing-technical-guidance) sets the conditions, including the difference between a house where you can acquire the freehold and a flat where you cannot. Do not copy the English "above 80%" rule across without reading that guidance.

### Scotland

Scottish shared-equity schemes, such as a government equity stake, are not the same thing as an English shared-ownership lease. A purchase is still a Scottish conveyance: missives, and Land and Buildings Transaction Tax to Revenue Scotland. Your solicitor must be qualified in Scots law. [Buying a house in Scotland](/blog/buying-a-house-in-scotland-conveyancing/) explains that process. Do not apply the SDLT market-value election to a Scottish purchase.

## Why is a new-build quote higher?

The developer's contract is not the form used on a second-hand house, and the deadline is often short, because the developer wants completion once the home is signed off. The conveyancer checks the contract against the reservation and any incentives, planning and who will look after the roads, the building warranty, and whether you are buying a freehold house or a lease with a rentcharge or a management company.

Snagging, the list of finishes to put right, is between you and the developer. It is not what the legal fee is for, though the contract may limit when you can raise those points.

A new lease is registered on the higher HM Land Registry column, as the table above says. In Northern Ireland a new title is registered under the 2014 Fees Order, on the value of the estate. In Scotland the fee is Registers of Scotland's, and the tax is LBTT.

None of this is a reason to pick the lowest legal fee without reading what is excluded. A new-build file still has to catch a rentcharge or a planning condition. [Questions to ask before you instruct](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-instructing-a-solicitor/) covers fixed fees and what would make them rise.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my shared-ownership quote higher than a normal purchase?

Because the conveyancer is dealing with a lease, a landlord and a lender with extra conditions, and has to explain a tax choice. There is no official surcharge. Compare the total, and ask which landlord fees are included.

### Should I pay stamp duty on the full value or on my share?

In England and Northern Ireland you can choose, if a qualifying body grants the lease. A market value election is one payment on the value in the lease, and nothing further when you staircase. Paying in stages means tax on the first share, and nothing more until you own over 80%. The election cannot be undone. Wales has its own Land Transaction Tax election.

### Is staircasing included in the purchase fee?

Not unless the quote says so. Buying a further share later is a new transaction, with its own legal fee and, depending on the tax choice you made at the start, possibly more tax.

### Why is a new-build solicitor more expensive?

The contract, the deadline and the new title take longer than a standard purchase. The Land Registry fee can also be higher, because a new lease does not get the reduced electronic fee. The developer's contribution, if there is one, should be in the contract.

### Do I pay the developer's solicitor as well as my own?

You pay your own conveyancer. Developers sometimes offer a contribution towards that fee. It should be written into what you sign. It rarely covers extras such as a gifted deposit or a separate lease extension.

### Does shared ownership work the same way in Scotland?

No. Scotland uses shared equity, which is a different arrangement, and the tax is LBTT rather than Stamp Duty Land Tax. The solicitor must be qualified in Scots law. The English market-value election does not apply.
