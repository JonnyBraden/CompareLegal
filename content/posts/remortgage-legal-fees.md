You need a solicitor or licensed conveyancer for a remortgage only if you are moving the mortgage to a new lender. A product transfer, where you take a new deal with your existing lender, usually needs no legal work, because that lender's charge is already registered. When you do switch, the legal work is narrower than a purchase: the old mortgage is paid off and the new lender's charge is registered. [Compare conveyancing fees](/conveyancing/), including remortgages, with VAT and disbursements in the total. How a full purchase quote is built up is in [conveyancing quotes in England and Wales](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/).

## Do you need a solicitor to remortgage?

**No, if you stay with your lender.** A product transfer does not change the registered charge. The lender handles the switch. Ask the lender to confirm that no solicitor is required before you assume there is nothing to pay.

**Yes, if you switch lenders.** The new lender needs its mortgage registered, and the old mortgage paid off and removed. A solicitor or licensed conveyancer does this, and often acts for both you and the new lender. In Scotland the solicitor must be qualified in Scots law. In Northern Ireland the solicitor must be qualified there.

## What should a remortgage quote include?

Checked 21 November 2026. There is no official scale for the legal fee.

| Part | What it is | Official figure, where there is one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal fee | Acting for you and, usually, the new lender: paying off the old mortgage and registering the new charge. | Set by the firm. |
| VAT | On the legal fee. | 20%. |
| Land registry fee | Registering the new lender's charge. In England and Wales this is an HM Land Registry Scale 2 fee, based on the amount secured. | See the bands below. |
| Searches, or search insurance | Many lenders accept insurance instead of full searches on a remortgage, which is cheaper and quicker. | No single national price. The quote should say which. |
| Identity checks and a bank transfer fee | Checks on each borrower, and sending the redemption money. | Set by the firm or the provider. Not a court fee. |
| Lender's valuation | Often arranged by the lender and charged on the mortgage offer, not inside the legal quote. | The lender's tariff, not a legal fee. |
| Stamp duty | Not charged on a straightforward remortgage with no change of owner. | See below. |

In England and Wales, HM Land Registry's Scale 2 fee for an electronic application to register a charge over the whole of a registered title is £20 up to £100,000, £30 from £100,001 to £200,000, £45 from £200,001 to £500,000, £65 from £500,001 to £1 million, and £140 above that. A postal application is higher, for example £100 where the amount secured is £200,001 to £500,000. The bands are in the Land Registration Fee Order 2024, in force since 9 December 2024. HM Land Registry is consulting on simplifying Scale 2 fees until 22 December 2026. The bands above are the ones in force.

Ask whether the old lender charges an administration fee to close the account. That comes from your own funds, or from the new mortgage, and it is not part of the conveyancer's legal fee.

## Are a lender's free legals worth taking?

Many lenders offer "free legals" on a remortgage, using a firm from their panel, or cashback towards your costs instead. Free legals can be good value for a straightforward switch. Before you accept, check:

- **What is not covered.** The package usually covers the standard work only. A leasehold property, a change of names on the title, or a defect in the title is often charged on top.
- **The service.** Panel firms handle high volumes. If you want a named contact, paying a modest fee may be worth it.
- **The interest rate.** A deal with no free legals can have a lower rate that saves more over the term than the legal fee costs.

Compare the total cost of the deal, including extras and the rate, rather than the legal fee alone.

## What if the names on the title are changing?

Adding or removing an owner at the same time is a **transfer of equity**, not a standard remortgage. It is more work, the fee is higher, and free-legal packages often exclude it. Depending on any cash paid and on the share of the mortgage the incoming owner takes on, it can also mean property tax. [Transfer of equity solicitor fees](/blog/transfer-of-equity-solicitor-fees/) covers that, including the cases where no tax is due. [SDLT, LTT and LBTT compared](/blog/stamp-duty-sdlt-ltt-lbtt-compared/) sets out the taxes themselves.

## How does a remortgage differ across the UK?

- **England and Wales.** The new mortgage is registered at HM Land Registry. Any solicitor or licensed conveyancer qualified in England and Wales can act. More on purchase quotes is in the [England and Wales quote guide](/blog/conveyancing-quote-explained-england-wales/).
- **Scotland.** A mortgage is a **standard security**, registered with Registers of Scotland. Your solicitor must be qualified in Scots law. [Buying a house in Scotland](/blog/buying-a-house-in-scotland-conveyancing/) explains how registration works there. The legal fee is still set by the firm.
- **Northern Ireland.** The mortgage is registered at Land Registry NI. Registering a charge is £110, or £90 if lodged electronically, and releasing the old charge is £20, under the Land Registry (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014. Unregistered titles go through the Registry of Deeds and can take longer. Your solicitor must be qualified in Northern Ireland. [Conveyancing costs in Northern Ireland](/blog/conveyancing-costs-northern-ireland/) has the full registration scale.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a solicitor to remortgage?

Only if you are switching to a new lender. A product transfer with your current lender usually needs no legal work. If you switch, a solicitor or licensed conveyancer registers the new charge and pays off the old mortgage.

### How long does a remortgage take?

Usually 3 to 5 weeks once the new lender has made its mortgage offer. It takes longer if there is a transfer of equity, a leasehold title or an unregistered title.

### Is it cheaper to use the lender's free legal service?

Often, on a simple remortgage. Compare the whole deal, including anything the package excludes and the interest rate, rather than the legal fee on its own.

### Do I pay stamp duty when I remortgage?

Not on a straightforward remortgage that does not change who owns the home. A transfer of equity can trigger Stamp Duty Land Tax, Land Transaction Tax or LBTT, depending on the cash paid and the share of the mortgage taken on.

### What does HM Land Registry charge to register the new mortgage?

A Scale 2 fee based on the amount secured. For an electronic application over the whole of a registered title, that is £45 where the mortgage is £200,001 to £500,000. Postal applications cost more. The fee is a disbursement, on top of the legal fee and VAT.

### Can I use any conveyancer for a remortgage in Scotland or Northern Ireland?

No. Scotland needs a solicitor qualified in Scots law, and Northern Ireland needs a solicitor qualified there. In England and Wales, a solicitor or a licensed conveyancer can act, and they need not be in the same city as the property.
