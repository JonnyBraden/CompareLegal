Solicitors' fees, side by side

Know the full price before you pick a solicitor.

Compare fixed fees for conveyancing, wills, probate and family law from firms across the UK. Every total includes VAT and disbursements, so the number you see is the number you pay. Prices are set by the firms themselves, it covers all four UK nations, and it's free to use with no sign-up.

What you can compare

  • Conveyancing: buying, selling or remortgaging a home. Fees are fixed and banded by the property price, plus searches and registration fees.
  • Wills & powers of attorney: a single or mirror will, or a power of attorney. Simple wills are usually a fixed fee; trusts and business assets cost more.
  • Probate & estates: getting a grant of probate (confirmation in Scotland) or having a solicitor administer the whole estate.
  • Divorce & family law: an uncontested divorce and a consent order recording your financial agreement, or advice on children arrangements.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need and where you are. For property, add the price, since conveyancing fees are set in value bands.
  2. Compare the full cost: each firm's legal fee, VAT and disbursements added up, so you compare like with like.
  3. Ask the firms you like for a written quote. They reply to you directly.

Three legal systems, one search

England and Wales: solicitors regulated by the SRA, property registered at HM Land Registry, and Land Transaction Tax for buyers in Wales. Scotland: solicitors regulated by the Law Society of Scotland, confirmation instead of probate, and LBTT for buyers. Northern Ireland: solicitors regulated by the Law Society of Northern Ireland, Stamp Duty Land Tax at England's rates, and enduring powers of attorney.
